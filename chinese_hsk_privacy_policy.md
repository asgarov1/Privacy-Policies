# Privacy Policy — Jlingo Chinese HSK

**Effective date:** November 16, 2026

**Developer:** Javid Asgarov

Jlingo Chinese HSK stores learning progress, custom learning entries, quiz
results, review schedules, preferences, streaks, and purchase entitlement
information locally on your device. The app does not send this learning data to a developer-operated
server. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

Optional premium subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.
Apple processes payment and transaction information; the developer does not
receive your payment-card details.

Pronunciation audio is bundled and plays locally. The app does not record your
microphone. Personal entries can use Apple’s system speech synthesis; voice
availability depends on your device. If you grant notification permission, the app
may schedule local study reminders, which you can disable in device settings.

A name entered for a completion certificate is processed on your device. If you
share or save a certificate, the chosen destination receives the information
you share. The certificate is not an official HSK result or accredited Chinese
qualification. If you email feedback, your email provider and the developer
receive the message and contact information you include.

Diagnostic logs are stored locally to help troubleshoot the app. You can view,
clear, or export them from Settings. Logs are not uploaded automatically; if
you share an export, the destination receives the information in that file.

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track
you across apps or websites. Deleting the app removes locally stored data;
backups may remain according to your Apple account settings. Purchases can be
restored through the app or your Apple account.

For privacy questions, contact **asgarovsoftware@gmail.com**.
