# Privacy Policy

**App:** Jlingo Japanese N1
**Effective date:** 2026-09-23
**Developer:** Javid Asgarov

## Learning data

Jlingo Japanese N1 stores learning progress, custom entries, quiz results,
preferences, streaks, and purchase-entitlement information on your device. It
does not send this learning data to a developer-operated server.

## Purchases, audio, and reminders

Optional premium purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services; Apple
processes payment information. Pronunciation audio plays locally and the app
does not record your microphone. If allowed, it may schedule local study
reminders, which you can disable in device settings.

## Certificates, sharing, and feedback

A certificate name is used locally. If you share a certificate, the destination
receives what you choose to share. It is not an official JLPT qualification.
Feedback email includes only the content and contact details you choose to send.

## Analytics and contact

The app has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you
across apps or websites. Deleting the app removes locally stored data, though
backups may remain under your backup settings. For questions, contact
**asgarovsoftware@gmail.com**.
