# Jlingo Japanese N4 Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 18, 2026  
**App:** Jlingo Japanese N4

Jlingo Japanese N4 stores your learning progress, saved learning entries, quiz results, and purchase entitlement status locally on your device so that the app can provide its learning features. The app does not operate its own account system and does not collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or tracking.

Optional subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy; the app does not receive your payment-card details.

If you use the system share sheet or contact support, the information you choose to share is handled by the destination service or email provider you select.

You can remove local learning data by deleting the app from your device. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
