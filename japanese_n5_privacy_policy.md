# Privacy Policy

**App:** Jlingo Japanese N5

**Effective date:** 2026-09-14

**Developer:** Javid Asgarov

## Learning data

Jlingo Japanese N5 stores learning progress, custom learning entries, quiz results,
preferences, streaks, and purchase entitlement information on your device. The
app does not send this learning data to a developer-operated server. Device
backups are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

## Purchases

Optional premium subscriptions use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.
Apple processes payment and transaction information. The developer does not
receive your payment-card details. Apple's practices are described in
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Audio and reminders

The course's pronunciation audio is bundled with the app and plays locally.
The app does not record your microphone. If you grant notification permission,
it may schedule local study reminders. You can disable these in device settings.

## Certificates, sharing, and feedback

A name entered for a course completion certificate is used to create that
certificate on your device. If you choose to share or save it, the destination
receives the information you share. A course certificate is not an official JLPT
qualification. If you choose to email feedback, your email provider and the
developer receive the message and the contact information you include.

## Analytics and children

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track
you across apps or websites. It does not knowingly collect children's personal
information through its learning features.

## Managing data

You can manage notification permissions through device settings. Deleting the
app removes its locally stored data; backups may remain according to your backup
settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account. Deleting the
app does not cancel a subscription.

## Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will include a revised effective date. For privacy
questions, contact **asgarovsoftware@gmail.com**.
