# Privacy Policy

- **App:** JBodyTrack
- **Effective date:** 2026-08-08
- **Developer:** Javid Asgarov

---

## 1. Overview

JBodyTrack helps you track weight, body mass index (BMI), body-fat percentage, and muscle percentage while keeping your data private.

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or body-tracking information. Data you enter in the App is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer or to an external server.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

The developer does not collect personal data from the App.

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Weight entries and entry dates | Track weight changes and calculate BMI | On-device only |
| Body-fat and muscle-percentage entries and entry dates | Track changes over time | On-device only |
| Profile details, including biological sex, birth date, height, waist, neck, and hip measurements | Calculate BMI and estimated body-fat percentage | On-device only |
| Calculation preferences | Remember your selected BMI and body-fat calculation methods | On-device only |
| Unit preference | Remember whether you use metric or U.S. customary units | On-device only |

This information is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

The App may use your App Store storefront country to choose an initial unit system. The developer does not receive or store this information.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks Premium features. Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple's App Store through StoreKit. The developer does not receive or store your payment card details. The App checks your App Store purchase entitlement to determine whether Premium features are unlocked.

Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs and does not share your body-tracking or app-usage data with third parties.

The App uses Apple's StoreKit service only to display, process, verify, and restore the Premium in-app purchase and to determine the App Store storefront as described above.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App does not transmit personal information to the developer, the developer has no personal data from child users to access.

---

## 6. Data Security

Your locally stored App data is protected by Apple's device security features and app sandboxing. The security of that data also depends on how you secure your device, including your use of a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where available.

The developer does not have access to your locally stored App data.

---

## 7. Your Choices

You can edit your profile and delete individual tracking entries within the App. You can remove the App's locally stored data by deleting the App from your device, subject to your device and backup settings.

Because the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data for the developer to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf.
