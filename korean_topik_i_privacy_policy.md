# Privacy Policy

**App:** Jlingo Korean TOPIK I

**Effective date:** 2026-09-27

**Developer:** Javid Asgarov

## Learning data

Jlingo Korean TOPIK I stores learning progress, custom learning entries, quiz
results, preferences, streaks, and purchase-entitlement information on your
device. The app does not send this learning data to a developer-operated server.
Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple account settings.

## Purchases

An optional one-time premium purchase uses Apple's StoreKit and App Store
services. Apple processes payment and transaction information. The developer
does not receive your payment-card details.

## Audio, reminders, and certificates

Pronunciation audio is bundled with the app and plays locally. The app does not
record your microphone. If you grant notification permission, it may schedule
local study reminders. A name entered for a completion certificate is used on
your device to create that certificate; it is not an official TOPIK result or
an accredited Korean qualification.

## Analytics and children

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track
you across apps or websites. It does not knowingly collect children's personal
information through its learning features.

## Managing data and contact

Deleting the app removes locally stored data, although backups may remain under
your Apple account settings. Restore completed purchases through the app or your
Apple account. For privacy questions, contact **asgarovsoftware@gmail.com**.
