## 1. Overview

Portuguese C1 ("the App") is designed with your privacy in mind. The App does not collect, store, or share personal information on external servers. All learning data remains on your device.

The App offers a premium subscription. Purchases are processed exclusively by Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your payment information. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from users of any age, including children under 13.

## 6. Data Security

Local app data is protected by your device's built-in security. The developer has no access to this data.

## 7. Your Rights

Because the App does not collect personal data, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, or delete. You can remove all local app data by deleting the App.
