# Privacy Policy — Snowboard Pocket Lessons

**Effective date:** November 23, 2026  
**Developer:** Javid Asgarov

Snowboard Pocket Lessons is an offline educational iOS app containing beginner snowboarding explanations, practice prompts, and knowledge checks.

## Information on your device

The app stores lesson progress, whether you have read a lesson, selected knowledge-check answers, and checked practice prompts locally on your device. This remembers unfinished practice and unlocks lessons in sequence. No account is required.

The app does not automatically transmit this information to the developer or any third party. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs, in-app purchases, or server connection. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, motion sensors, or photos. Practice is self-reported; the app does not record or monitor your physical activity.

## Optional session logs

The information screen includes Logs for troubleshooting. The app keeps up to 200 timestamped app events in memory for the current session, such as lesson IDs opened, page changes, checklist or answer updates, and lesson completion. Logs do not contain the selected quiz answers or device identifiers. They reset when the app process restarts and can be cleared without changing lesson progress. They are not saved to disk or included in backups.

If you choose Share logs, the system share sheet lets you send the log text, including the app version and operating-system version, to a recipient or app you select. No logs are sent automatically. Shared copies are handled by the recipient or service you choose.

## Backups and external links

Your local app data may be included in device backups according to your Apple settings. Apple provides these services under its own privacy policy, and the developer cannot access your backups.

Opening an instructional source or the privacy-policy link launches an external website in your browser. Those websites receive normal web requests and operate under their own privacy policies. No lesson progress or quiz answers are included in these links.

## Your choices

All lessons work without an internet connection. Deleting the app removes its local progress, subject to your device and backup settings. There is no developer-held personal dataset or account to delete or export.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data handling changes. Contact **office@asgarovsoftware.com** with privacy questions.
