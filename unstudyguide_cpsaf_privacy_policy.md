# Privacy Policy

- **App:** Unstudy Guide CPSA-F
- **Effective date:** 2026-07-26
- **Developer:** Javid Asgarov

---

## 1. Overview

Unstudy Guide CPSA-F is designed to help users study for the CPSA-F exam while keeping their data private. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties.

All study data created while using the App stays on your device.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

The developer does not collect personal data from the App.

The App may store the following information locally on your device only:

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|------|---------|---------|
| Lectures marked as studied | Track your learning progress | On-device only |
| Lectures marked for repeat | Help you revisit selected material | On-device only |
| Quiz attempt history | Show previous quiz results and progress | On-device only |
| Quiz scores and completion times | Show your quiz performance | On-device only |
| Language preference | Remember your selected app language | On-device only |

This information is not sent to the developer or to any external server.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases

The App currently does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions.

If in-app purchases are added in the future, purchases would be processed by Apple's App Store. The developer would not receive or store your payment card details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. The App does not share app usage data with third parties.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal data for the developer to access from child users.

---

## 6. Data Security

Because the App stores study data only on your device, that data is protected by your device's built-in security features, such as your passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and system-level app sandboxing.

The developer does not have access to your locally stored app data.

---

## 7. Your Choices

You can reset your study progress from within the App where this option is available. You can also remove all locally stored app data by deleting the App from your device.

Because the developer does not collect or store your personal data, there is no personal data for the developer to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf.

---

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
